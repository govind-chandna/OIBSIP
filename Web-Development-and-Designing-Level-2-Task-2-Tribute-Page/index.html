About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's space and defence programmes.

He was widely admired for his dedication to education, science and inspiring young people to dream big and work hard to achieve their goals.

His simple personality, knowledge and vision made him one of the most respected personalities in India.

Important Achievements

1931

Born on 15 October in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1960s

Joined India's space programme and contributed to important aerospace projects.

1980

Played a major role in the development of India's satellite launch vehicle programme.

2002

Became the 11th President of India.

2015

Passed away while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.
